## ⚽ About Football Livescore Tv

**Football Livescore Tv** is a football-focused application that helps users stay informed with:

- Match schedules and fixtures  
- Live scores and match updates  
- Football coverage across multiple leagues  
- Simple, lightweight user experience  

The app is designed for accessibility and ease of use, without unnecessary complexity.

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Transparency

Football Livescore Tv follows clear privacy and advertising standards:

- 🔒 No account registration required  
- 📵 No direct collection of personal user information  
- 📢 Advertising handled via Google AdMob  
- 📜 ads.txt publicly available for ad transparency  

Full details are available in the app’s privacy policy.
